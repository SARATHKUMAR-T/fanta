# Code of conduct

Fanta follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the code, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/SARATHKUMAR-T). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block anyone who breaks these rules.
